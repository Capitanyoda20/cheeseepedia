I was one of those who wanted them to save Willowdale too (because of the statue, Jasper's PTT mask and the Star Cap logo) when it was confirmed I was happy, and it seemed like a great idea to move the road stage there. Unfortunately it broke and according to the corporation it didn't feel right to recreate it (probably to save costs), I already thought it was an exaggeration to say that the Mississauga robots were "unsalvageable", but that was worse, at least they said they were going to make changes, just so that it would be just Jasper's hat and the new carpet (although this one was more necessary since the previous one had a hole in it), in Mark's reddit post, almost everyone agreed that they had to put Munch's PTT mask and Jasper's banjo (now extinct) they did a complete remodel they kept nothing but the awning that seems to have been there for a long time, the stage and some art they had before with the statue I'm the only one who liked the selection and having it, although I feel that the other side should have also and at least put the star cap logo there. On top of that, the animatronics were in poor condition, it felt very rushed and desperate to make the fans happy, ending up being the worst legacy store
